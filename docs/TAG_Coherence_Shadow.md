# TAG — Coherence Shadow Principle

## Overview
Higher-dimensional coherence cannot be directly perceived by lower-dimensional observers.
Instead, it appears as **vibration**, fluctuation, or stochastic motion.
This document summarizes how each dimensional totelevation expresses itself as a lower-dimensional “shadow.”

## Dimensional Coherence Ladder (0D → 6D)
| Dim | Name          | Nature of Coherence                         | What the Lower Dim Perceives |
|-----|----------------|----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 0D  | Potential      | Infinite undirected energy                   | Nothing (no frame to detect) |
| 1D  | Time Vector    | First direction; energy motion               | “Flow” / change              |
| 2D  | Aether         | Subatomic substrate; quantum coherence       | Atomic vibration              |
| 3D  | Terra          | Gravity; mass stability; shared fields       | Structured matter            |
| 4D  | Numen          | Life coherence; time-extended pattern        | Metabolic “vibration” / life |
| 5D  | Lima           | Will / intent; directed coherence            | Thought / impulse patterns   |
| 6D  | Corporeal      | Collective agency; societal coherence        | Cultural “oscillations”      |

## Coherence Shadow Rule
A dimension **n+1** expresses itself to dimension **n** as:
- oscillation
- jitter
- noise with structure
- periodic or quasi-periodic signatures

This is because the new orthogonal axis of coherence is inaccessible to the lower-dimensional observer.

## Upward Totelevation
Upward coherence produces new degrees of freedom.

Examples:
- 2D → 3D: matter gains gravitational stability
- 3D → 4D: life emerges as distributed 4D coherence
- 4D → 5D: intent forms as directed higher-order coherence

To lower dimensions, each appears as vibrational patterns.

## Downward Totelevation (Black Hole Case)
Coherence collapse (gravity override) produces the illusion of **dimensional reduction**.
To us, black hole interiors appear “2D,” but this is a projection:
- true state = n+1 coherence axis
- perceived state = n–1 flattened surface

Downward totelevation looks like loss of dimension but is actually **coherence exceeding the time vector** and re-basing into a new orthogonal axis we cannot perceive.

## Summary
- Every dimension casts a “coherence shadow” on the one below it.
- Life is the vibration of 4D coherence as seen from 3D.
- Atoms are the vibration of 2D coherence as seen from 3D.
- Black holes represent downward totelevation: infinite coherence appearing as dimensional loss.
