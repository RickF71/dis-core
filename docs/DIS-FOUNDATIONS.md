## Foundational Force: Caritas

**Definition:**
Caritas is the sustaining flow of life and coherence within DIS.
It represents *love through service*—the active current of care that keeps every domain alive.

**Principle:**
All structure requires flow; all flow is Caritas.
Caritas is not a token or resource, but an *ongoing act of support* that circulates through people, domains, and systems.

**Properties:**
- **Dynamic:** Caritas exists only while moving. When it stops, it decays back into potential.
- **Non-Hoardable:** It cannot be owned or stockpiled; its value lies in continuity, not accumulation.
- **Transformative:** As Caritas moves, it manifests in visible civic and creative actions, measurable as DisCoin flow.
- **Universal:** Every domain, from Terra to Lima, depends on Caritas for existence and coherence.

**Equation:**
`Infrastructure + Stewardship + Consent → Flow(Caritas) → Continuity of Life`

**Canonical Reference:**
`flow.caritas.v1`

**Purpose:**
Caritas defines the moral thermodynamics of DIS. It is the living proof that freedom and love are not opposites—they are the same current seen from different directions.
