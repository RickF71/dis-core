# DIS Coherence Principle
### (2-3-4D Triad)

**Summary**
Every domain in DIS sustains itself through a closed triadic loop of **Policy → Data → Act**.

---

## 1. Structure of the Triad

| Layer | Dimension | Role | Function |
|------|-----------|------|----------|
| **Policy** | 2 D | Structural field | Defines lawful order and damps entropy. |
| **Data** | 3 D | Manifest form | Holds the living state shaped by policy. |
| **Act** | 4 D | Temporal motion | Expresses behavior through time, feeding results back into the system. |

---

## 2. Human Coupling

A person enters a domain through **Jikka**, linking their own triad with the domain’s:

    (person.policy → person.data → person.act)
                 ⇅ Jikka
    (domain.policy → domain.data → domain.act)

This coupling injects intent, receives feedback, and completes the coherence circuit—
**law shapes life, life animates time, time refines law.**

---

## 3. Conditions of Coherence

A coherent domain:

1. **Stands Alone** – maintains structural sovereignty.
2. **Connects Through Jikka** – all exchange occurs under consent.
3. **Balances Policy, Data, and Act** – homeostasis between structure, form, and motion.

---

## 4. Principle

> The persistence of this closed feedback—
> **entropy constrained by structure, expression governed by consent**—
> is what allows DIS to remain both **stable and alive.**
